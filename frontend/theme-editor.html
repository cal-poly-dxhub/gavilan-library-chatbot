Pick the colour, font and starter questions here, then download the finished settings file. Nothing changes until you upload that file - the last step is the same WidgetThemeUpload link as always.

Highlight colour

Used for the header, the launcher, Send, your visitors' own chat bubbles, and links. The text that sits on it is not a setting: the widget picks black or white on its own, whichever reads better on your colour.

Not a colour the widget can read. Use a hex value: six digits like #1e4b8f, or three like #a13.

Font

Only the typeface changes. Sizes and spacing are fixed so the widget stays readable when visitors zoom. No fonts are downloaded, so the choices are limited to what every Mac and Windows machine already has.

Starter questions

The example questions offered before anyone types, up to four per language, 120 characters each. Leave every Spanish field empty and Spanish-speaking visitors see your English questions - nothing is machine-translated. Leave both languages empty to keep the built-in questions.
